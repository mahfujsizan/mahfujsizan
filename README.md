### Overview

I work at the intersection of satellite remote sensing, physical hydrology, and machine learning. My focus centers on cloud-based spatial pipelines in **Google Earth Engine (GEE)** and predictive models for hydroclimatic hazards, groundwater potential assessment, and atmospheric environmental dynamics.

- 🛰️ **Earth Observation**: Large-scale processing of multispectral and radar imagery (Sentinel-1/2/5P, Landsat 5–9, MODIS, CHIRPS, SRTM DEM).
- 💧 **Hydroinformatics**: Spatial multi-criteria evaluation, catchment hydrology, flood hazard modeling, and aquifer potential assessment.
- 🤖 **Applied Machine Learning**: Random Forest, Support Vector Machines, Artificial Neural Networks, and CA-Markov cellular automata for spatial modeling and land-change simulation.
